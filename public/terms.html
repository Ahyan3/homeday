Who runs Homeday

Homeday is run by Ryan Francis Romano in the Philippines. For anything on this page, email ahyanromano02@gmail.com.

Using Homeday

  • Making, saving and sharing a timeline is free.
  • Only upload photos you took or have permission to use.
  • Don't put anyone's private details, or anything hateful or explicit, in a timeline or a guestbook. We remove timelines and messages that do.
  • The edit link works like a key. Anyone who has it can change that timeline, so keep it private.
  • Weather, moon and song facts are for fun. They come from public data and can occasionally be off.

Privacy

We keep only what the site needs, and we never sell it. We handle personal data in line with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act 10173).

  • What we keep: your pet's name, kind and dates, the town or city you enter, the photos you upload, and the names and messages people leave in guestbooks.
  • When you buy: PayMongo takes the payment and shares your name, email and, for printed items, your delivery address with us. We never see your card or e-wallet details.
  • Who can see a timeline: anyone who has its link. Timelines aren't listed on the site, and we ask search engines not to show them.
  • Where it's stored: on Supabase (database and photos). To look up the weather and the place, we send the town name to OpenStreetMap and its map coordinates to NASA POWER, without your name or your pet's.
  • Deleting: email us the timeline's link and we'll delete the timeline, its photos and its guestbook within 7 days.

Payments and refunds

  • Prices are in Philippine pesos. You pay through PayMongo with GCash, Maya, QR Ph or card.
  • A digital keepsake unlocks within a few minutes of payment. If it doesn't, or something on it is wrong, email us. We'll fix it, or refund you in full if we can't.
  • Not happy with a digital keepsake? Email us within 7 days of buying and we'll refund it.
  • Printed items, when we offer them, are made to order. If one arrives damaged or wrong, we'll replace it or refund it.

Changes to this page

If we change these terms, we'll update the date at the top. Big changes will also be announced on our Facebook page.
