Refund policy

If something isn't right, we'll fix it or give your money back. Email ahyanromano02@gmail.com with your PayMongo receipt or the timeline's link.

Last updated 21 November 2026

Digital keepsakes

  • Changed your mind? Ask within 7 days of buying and we'll refund you in full. No reason needed.
  • Didn't unlock, or something is wrong on it? We'll fix it. If we can't, we'll refund you in full, at any time.
  • Bought as a gift? The person who paid can ask for the refund.

Printed items

Prints and books are made to order, so we can't take them back if you change your mind. If one arrives damaged or wrong, email us a photo within 14 days of delivery and we'll replace it or refund it in full. You don't need to send it back.

How you're paid back

  • We send the refund to the card or e-wallet you paid with, within 3 working days of agreeing it. If you paid by GCash or Maya QR code, it goes back to the same GCash or Maya account.
  • How long it then takes to show up depends on your bank or e-wallet.
  • You get back what you paid, in the currency you paid in. If you paid from abroad, your bank's exchange rate on the day may make the amount differ slightly.

Your other rights

This policy adds to your rights under Philippine consumer law, and the consumer law of your own country if you live elsewhere. It doesn't take any away.
