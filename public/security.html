Security

How we protect your timelines and payments, and how to tell us if you find a problem.

Payments

  • You pay on PayMongo's own checkout page. Homeday never sees or stores card numbers or e-wallet logins.
  • PayMongo confirms each card payment with a signed message. We check the signature, and we only unlock a keepsake if the amount and currency match the order.
  • For QR code payments, we find your reference number in our own account and check the amount before we unlock anything. Each reference number can only be used once.

Timelines and photos

  • Each timeline's address is random and isn't listed anywhere, and we ask search engines not to show timelines.
  • Edit links contain a long random key. We store only a scrambled (hashed) form of it, so even we can't read your key.
  • Photos are re-saved in your browser before upload. That removes hidden data such as the GPS location where the photo was taken.
  • Photo addresses are random. Anyone with a photo's address can open it, so don't upload anything sensitive.

The website and database

  • Every page loads over HTTPS, and browsers are told never to use an unencrypted connection.
  • The site runs no advertising, analytics or other outside scripts. Fonts are served from our own site.
  • A strict content security policy tells browsers to run only our own code, which blocks most injected scripts.
  • Nobody can read or change the database tables directly. The site can only ask a small set of functions, and each one checks who's asking and what they ask for.
  • Limits on new timelines and guestbook messages slow down spam and abuse.

What you can do

  • Treat your edit link like a password. Don't post it publicly.
  • Share the timeline link, not the edit link, with friends.
  • We will never ask for your card details or a one-time PIN by email or message. Anyone who does isn't us.

Report a security problem

Email ahyanromano02@gmail.com with "Security" in the subject. Tell us what you found and how to see it. We'll reply within 3 days and keep you updated until it's fixed.

While you look, please don't open, change or delete other people's timelines or orders, don't slow the site down, and give us time to fix the problem before you tell anyone else. If you follow these rules in good faith, we won't take legal action against you.

Our contact details are also in security.txt.
