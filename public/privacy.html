Privacy policy

We keep only what Homeday needs to work, we never sell it, and there are no ads or trackers. We follow the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act 10173), and we give the same rights to visitors from every country.

What stays in your browser

Homeday sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising scripts. Your browser's local storage keeps your edit links, the last timeline you opened and your currency choice, so you don't have to enter them again. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.

Who handles your data for us

  • Supabase stores the database and photos, on servers in Singapore.
  • Vercel or Cloudflare host the website and see standard web logs.
  • PayMongo takes payments.
  • Resend sends reminder emails, if you turned them on.
  • OpenStreetMap receives the town name you type, and NASA POWER its map coordinates, to look up the place and weather. Neither gets your name or your pet's.

Some of these companies are outside the Philippines, so your data may be handled in other countries. We only use services that protect it, and we never sell or rent your data to anyone.

Who can see a timeline

Anyone who has its link, including its photos and guestbook. Timelines aren't listed on the site, and we ask search engines not to show them. Don't add anything you wouldn't want a stranger to see if the link were passed on.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what we keep about you and give you a copy
  • correct it
  • delete it (we delete a timeline, its photos and its guestbook within 7 days)
  • stop using it, for example to stop reminder emails

Email ahyanromano02@gmail.com with the timeline's link or the email address involved. We reply within 15 days. If you're unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the National Privacy Commission of the Philippines or to the data protection authority where you live.

Children

Homeday isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has given us personal details, email us and we'll delete them.

Keeping it safe

See how we protect your data. If we ever have a breach that puts your data at risk, we'll tell you and the National Privacy Commission as the law requires.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use data you've already given us, we'll ask first.
