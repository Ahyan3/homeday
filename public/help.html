Making a timeline

Is it free?
Yes. Making, saving and sharing a timeline is free, with no account. Only the keepsakes cost money.
What do I need?
Just the day your pet came home. A birthday, a town or city, and photos make the timeline richer.
How do I change it later?
When you save, you get a private edit link. Open it to change anything. The browser you saved from also remembers it.
I lost my edit link.
Open the timeline in the browser you made it with, and the Edit button should still be there. If it isn't, email us the timeline's link and when you made it, and we'll help.
Which photos can I add?
Up to 12 JPEG, PNG, WebP or HEIC photos. They're made smaller in your browser before they're uploaded, which also removes hidden location data.

The facts on your timeline

Where does the weather come from?
From NASA POWER, which has daily weather for anywhere on Earth since 1981. Earlier dates show "No record". Visitors in the United States see Fahrenheit; everyone else sees Celsius.
Which song is it?
The number one on the US Billboard Hot 100 in the week your pet came home. The chart started in August 1958.
How is the moon worked out?
It's calculated from the date, so it works for any day.
Something looks wrong.
The facts come from public data and are for fun, so the odd one can be off. If the town is wrong, edit the timeline and type the town with its province or country.

Sharing and privacy

Who can see my timeline?
Anyone you send the link to. Timelines aren't listed on the site, and we ask search engines not to show them.
Can I remove a guestbook message?
Yes. Open the timeline with your edit link and hide the message.
How do I delete my timeline?
Email us its link. We delete the timeline, its photos and its guestbook within 7 days. More about your data.

Homeday reminders

What are they?
One email a year, a week before your pet's homeday. Turn them on from Share on your saved timeline. Memorial timelines never get them.
How do I stop them?
Use the "Stop these emails" link at the bottom of any reminder. It works straight away.

Keepsakes and payment

What do I get?
A poster and a certificate made from your timeline, ready to print at home or at any print shop, or to save as a PDF.
How do I pay?
Right now, by GCash or Maya: scan our QR code, pay ₱699, and send us the reference number from your receipt. We check each payment by hand, usually within a few hours and always within a day, then email you the keepsake link. Card payments through PayMongo, including from abroad, open soon.
I'm outside the Philippines. Which currency am I charged in?
Prices show in US dollars. Until our dollar payments are switched on, your card is charged the peso price and your bank converts it, so the amount on your statement can differ a little. The checkout page always shows the exact amount before you pay. You can switch the prices shown under the keepsakes.
I paid, but the keepsake is still a preview.
If you paid by GCash or Maya QR, we unlock it once we've checked your payment, usually within a few hours; the keepsake page says when it's being checked. If you paid by card, it unlocks within a minute. Still locked after a day? Email us your receipt.
Can I get a refund?
Yes, within 7 days for a digital keepsake. See refunds.
