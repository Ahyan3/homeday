        For the ones who are gone

        A memorial timeline is quieter. It ends on the day you said goodbye, counts the days you had, and leaves out everything about what comes next. Friends can sign its guestbook.

        What we promise

        • No countdown timers, no discounts that expire, no reminder emails about a pet you lost.
        • The memorial timeline and its guestbook are free, the same as every other timeline.
        • You can make one for a grieving friend and send them the edit link to finish it.
